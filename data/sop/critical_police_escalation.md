# Trigger conditions

Treat the case as CRITICAL for immediate human review when available information indicates an imminent threat to life or safety, ongoing assault, serious injury, weapon threat, credible threat of violence, confinement, forced displacement, a self-harm emergency, or retaliation/intimidation connected to a possible atrocity report. Risk tier is an assistive triage signal, not a substitute for human judgment; escalate whenever the reported facts warrant it even if a score is unavailable.

# Immediate operator actions

- Do not leave the caller waiting for routine queue processing. Alert the designated on-call supervisor and follow the approved local emergency contact protocol.
- Confirm the caller's current location only if safe to ask; ask whether they can reach a safer public place or trusted person. Do not instruct a caller to confront an alleged perpetrator, collect evidence, travel alone, or take any action that increases danger.
- If emergency services are contacted or a police handoff is made, share only the minimum necessary information and record the time, destination, reference number if provided, caller consent/limitations, and whether contact was completed.
- If the caller cannot speak safely, follow the platform's approved silent-contact and supervisor procedures; do not assume that a message is safe to send or that the caller can receive a callback.

# Protection and confidentiality

For possible SC/ST Act matters, section 15A places responsibility on the State to arrange protection against intimidation, coercion, violence, and threats of violence. Record the reported protection concern and route it for urgent human/legal review; do not promise protection, relocation, arrest, or a court order. Restrict the record to authorised personnel and do not reveal a caller's address, phone number, or witness details more widely than necessary for the authorised emergency referral.

# Stabilisation and handoff

Remain focused on immediate safety until the human operator or emergency authority takes over. Then record the facts reported, risk indicators, steps taken, agencies contacted, outcome of each contact, consent boundaries, safe follow-up method, and the named handoff owner. If contact fails, keep the case in urgent human review and document the failure; never mark a case resolved solely because an automated notification was sent.

# Sources

- India Code, *Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989*, section 15A (protection and rights of victims and witnesses).
- State/UT emergency, police, and protection procedures must be checked and followed for each location.

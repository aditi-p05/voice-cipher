# Critical risk triage

- Action: Present the case to the designated human operator for immediate review.
- Action: Record the operator's confirmation or override in the case audit record.

# High risk triage

- Action: Queue the case for priority human operator review.
- Action: Show the retrieved evidence summary and SOP citation to the operator.

# Moderate risk triage

- Action: Present available support options to the human operator for review.

# Low risk triage

- Action: Keep the case available for routine human operator review.
